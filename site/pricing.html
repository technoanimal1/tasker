Credits

How credits work.

One simple unit for everything the tool renders. What you see in the studio is exactly what your monthly statement says.

1 credit1 thumbnail — one game rendered in one size. 3:4, 2:3, 47:66 or 16:9 — same price, no surcharges.
4 creditsOne game in all four sizes. A 1,000-game lobby in every size is 4,000 credits — that's it.
0 creditsFree: CSS light animations, re-exports of any rendered thumbnail, format changes (PNG / WebP / AVIF) and template tweaks.
10 creditsAI animation — a perfectly looped, alpha-matted animated thumbnail. About $1–2 at pack rates, not $8.
